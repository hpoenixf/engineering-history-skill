# Material workflow

## Candidate to canonical entry

Promote when there is a non-obvious, reusable judgment, enough observed outcome to state its limits and no existing topic already covering it. Reuse an entry when the contribution is merely another check of the same mechanism.

Keep intended outcomes, observations, interpretations and unknowns distinct. Passing tests establish their checked behavior, not production adoption, live model quality or long-term benefit. Historical results retain their original date and version.

## Daily view

Group by stable topic, not one card per task. Keep the new judgment, result/limit, independent interview/sharing value and links to canonical entries. Select a small set of important topics and merge repeated workstreams. This view should be substantially quicker to read than its sources.

## Topic view

Keep a core principle, stages that changed understanding and representative canonical entries. Update for a new topic, changed principle, meaningful stage or better case. Test counts and repeated closes alone are not topic evolution.

## Protection

Existing daily/type files are protected outside one ordered AUTO-REFINE region; unmarked files are protected in full. A snapshot is a before-state, not permission to overwrite concurrent edits. Mechanical validation makes no semantic-quality claim.
